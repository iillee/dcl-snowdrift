## The scarcity-model pivot (end of session \u2014 NOT YET IN THE DOCS)

Late in the session the linear-tech-collapse worry ("once you have pine, why bother with deadwood?") led to a discussion about *late-run stagnation* ("once you have pine, why explore at all?"). After running through several solves (spatial asymmetry; fuel-tempo asymmetry; seasonal reveals; aurora/meteor events; tree-lifecycle-with-Spring-regrowth), owner surfaced the setting inconsistency that unlocked the actual answer.

### The setting fix
**Cryogenian = Snowball Earth = frozen year-round.** There is no warm spring in this world. Plants don't grow. My tree-regrowth model imported a real-world seasonal cycle that doesn't apply. **Rejected.**

### The finite-wood model (locked in principle, not yet in docs)
- **Trees are pre-freeze remnants** \u2014 dead standing timber from before the world froze. Not a living ecosystem.
- **The map has a fixed, finite wood budget per world.** Every tree has a fixed per-tree budget (3\u20135 logs); no tree regrows within a run.
- **Every log burned subtracts from the world's total.** Full stop.
- **World reset (hearth death) restores the world.** New seed = new pre-freeze layout = fresh finite budget. The reset *is* the "regrowth."
- **Runs are terminal.** Community's job is to make a dying supply last long enough to survive winter and long enough to matter.

### Why this landed
- Fits Cryogenian tone perfectly (I should have caught this earlier).
- Simplifies the fuel model (no respawn timers to tune).
- Makes community discipline mechanically real (which biome do we deplete first?).
- Kills late-run stagnation without event systems (scarcity itself is the pressure).
- Maps 1:1 to the v2 hook \u2014 the ancients built the volcano ignition network because they knew wood ran out. Your v1 run of "burn through the wood" is exactly what compels the v2 arc of "wake the volcanoes."

### The v1 \u2192 v1.5 \u2192 v2 scarcity ladder (proposed spine for the pitch)
- **v1** \u2014 wood scarcity. Master finite supply within a run.
- **v1.5** \u2014 coal / peat / ancient stores discovered at fixed procgen sites. Rarer than pine, longer-burning. Natural next tier when wood scarcity bites.
- **v2** \u2014 volcanoes. Wake the ancients' ignition network. Thaw the world. The loop's terminal answer.

### Tree harvest mechanic

**LOCKED 2026-09-22 (owner):** intentional torch-melt-down at the base in v1. Player sustains torch heat at the trunk footing until the tree falls (~3 s target, playtest-tunable). Reframes as "warm the tree until it falls" \u2014 an extension of the melt verb, not a new verb. Adds agency over *when* to fell (strategic: fell now for logs, or leave standing as a landmark / future reserve).

Downed-trunk gather visual \u2014 decision deferred to build:
- **(a) Shatter-on-fall:** the fallen tree resolves immediately into N pickup-able log entities on the ground.
- **(b) Progressive chunking:** the fallen trunk stays as one model; each pickup removes a chunk from the model until gone. More diegetic; higher art/rig cost.

Depleted trees leave **permanent stumps** as visible scars for the rest of the run. A Day 80 world looks lived-in.

## Open questions blocking the finite-wood model going into the docs

These are the four questions I posed at end of session; owner did not answer them explicitly before switching to "record this in a doc." They need answers before the doc integration pass.

1. ~~**Auto-fell on last pickup vs. intentional torch-melt-down for v1.**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-22 (owner):** intentional torch-melt-down at the tree base in v1. Sustained torch heat at the base melts the trunk footing until the tree falls \u2014 an extension of the melt verb, not a new verb. Once fallen, logs are gathered from the downed trunk. Two sub-options for the downed-trunk visual, decision deferred to build:
    - **(a) Shatter-on-fall:** the fallen tree resolves immediately into N pickup-able log entities on the ground.
    - **(b) Progressive chunking:** the fallen trunk stays as one model; each pickup removes a chunk from the model until it's gone. Reads more diegetic; slightly higher art/rig cost.
    Either way: no auto-fell, no new verb, tree footprint persists as a stump after depletion.
2. ~~**Mid-run scarcity pressure intensity.**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-22 (owner):** generous v1. The wood budget is comfortable within a normal-length run; solstice failure and mismanagement remain the primary loss vectors. Scarcity pressure emerges naturally across multi-solstice runs \u2014 by the 2nd\u20133rd solstice, if a community gets that far, the finite budget starts biting and *forces outward exploration to find alternate fuel sources*. This is exactly the on-ramp to v1.5 coal / ancient stores: the game's own scarcity curve tells the community when it's time for the next tier.
3. ~~**Coal / ancient stores as the v1.5 headline pitch feature.**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-22 (owner):** coal and ancient stores are **expansion (v1.5+), not v1**. v1 ships with wood only \u2014 two tree types (deadwood, pinewood), the tree-mining mechanic (torch-melt-fell + downed-trunk gather), and the finite budget. Balancing tree placement, per-tree budgets, and total-world budget across the 500 m ring is already enough tuning work for v1. Coal / ancient stores remains the CSP v1.5 pitch headline ("the game's own scarcity tells you when it's time"), but is out of v1 scope.
4. ~~**Promote "the world is finite; every burn subtracts" to a named GDD pillar?**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-22 (owner):** yes. Promote to a named pillar. Pillar 7 ("biomes are where resources renew") is now inconsistent with the finite-wood model and must be revised or replaced in the same edit \u2014 see "Pillar 7 tension" below (final wording still owner call: reword to *"biomes are where resources concentrate"* vs. replace outright with the finite-wood principle).
